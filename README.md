Breach 2D is a top-down tactical shooter inspired by close-quarters breach-and-clear games. The maps are dark. You carry a flashlight and glow sticks, and your night vision needs a battery you must find and wire in. Move through 8 huge maps with 30 to 60 hostiles, lock down every objective, and bring your squad home alive.

Choose your operator

Vanguard: Rifle and a mirror gun for peeking around corners.

Doc: SMG and medkits that heal you or your squad.

Ghost: A suppressed pistol and knife, plus a cloak that hides you and your squad for 10 seconds.

Bulwark: A heavy shield that blocks frontal fire, at the cost of speed and firepower.

Ram: A shotgun that blows doors off, plus breaching charges.

Ajax: A burst rifle and a light rescue shield you can raise or place down as cover.

Features

8 large maps: House, Warehouse, Office, Nightclub, Hospital, Prison, Mansion and Bunker. Each has randomized enemies and crates every mission, and its own objective such as rescuing hostages or disarming bombs.

6 enemy types: Thug, Gunman, Rusher, Marksman, Heavy and Commander. They hunt you through the map and call for help.

Command your squad: Hold, follow, stack at a door, breach and clear, or hold fire. Pick a class for each squadmate: Assault, Medic or Marksman. Upgrade them with the points you earn.

Door breaching: Kick doors open for slow motion, stun and knockback. Reinforced doors take two kicks.

Realistic injuries on Hard and Nightmare: Head, chest, arm and leg hits each have their own effect, including screen blur and unstable aim.

Deep loadouts: Pick weapons, a helmet, a vest, bandages, and the AN/PVS-31A or GPNVG-18 night vision, all within a limited number of gear slots.

Cosmetic shop: Preview uniforms, helmets, gun skins and tracers on your operator before buying.

Strict grades: Missions are graded S to F. An S needs no squad deaths, no damage taken, and all hostiles down in under 4 minutes.

Play anywhere: It works with a keyboard and mouse or touch controls, with an auto-shoot option for mobile.
